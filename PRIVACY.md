# Privacy Policy for LDB Player

Last updated: 08/14/2026

## Scope
This privacy policy applies to the official LDB Player application distributed through the Microsoft Store (version 1.2.0 and later).

## Information We Collect
LDB Player does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

- No user accounts  
- No analytics or tracking  
- No access to your files or personal data beyond the video files you explicitly choose to play  

## Update Mechanism
The official Microsoft Store version receives updates through the Microsoft Store. No personal data is transmitted during this process.

(Legacy GitHub releases of version 1.1.6 and earlier optionally checked for updates via GitHub. That check only sent the app version number and is no longer used in the current Store version.)

## Data from Third Parties
The app uses VLC media player (libVLC) and PySide6 for video playback and the user interface. Any data handling is limited to the video files you choose to play and standard system interaction required for desktop integration.

## Contact
For any questions regarding privacy, please contact the developer via:  
https://github.com/kakao90g/ldb_player  

or through the Discord community linked in the application.

This policy may be updated in the future. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
